Privacy Policy

Cafe Stack Pop · last updated 28 July 2026

Cafe Stack Pop doesn't ask you to make an account, and it doesn't collect your name, your email address, or your phone number. Your game progress stays on your phone.

In fact the game collects nothing at all. This policy explains what that means in practice, and covers the Cafe Stack Pop iOS app, published by Daler Soliev.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, hearts, cats, season progress, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We don't upload them, and we can't see them.

The practical consequence: if you delete the app, that progress is gone, and it won't follow you to a new phone. There's no server-side copy for us to restore.

Advertising

This version of the game contains no advertising. There is no ad network in the app, no advertising identifier is read, and you are not tracked across other companies' apps or websites.

If we add advertising in a future version we will update this page before that version ships, and the App Store privacy label will change with it.

Analytics and crash reporting

There aren't any. The game sends no analytics events and uses no crash-reporting service, so we have no record of how you play, when you play, or what device you play on.

The game makes two kinds of network request, neither of which carries an identifier. The first is an optional check for updated event dates and prices; if it fails, the game uses the values built into the app. The second downloads the game's typefaces from a public content delivery network (jsDelivr) the first time you launch it, which means that network sees your device's IP address, as any web request would. If it fails, the game falls back to your device's system font.

Neither request tells us anything about you, and the game remains fully playable with no connection at all.

Purchases

All purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells us only whether it succeeded. We never see your card number, your Apple Account credentials, or your billing address.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You manage and cancel them in your iPhone's Settings, not in the game — see the support page for the steps.

Children

Cafe Stack Pop is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but it isn't directed at children specifically and it isn't in the App Store's Kids Category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the game contains purchases, parents may want to turn on Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions to require approval before any purchase is made.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored about you, because all of it lives on your device.

There is no account to close and nothing on our side to erase, because there is nothing on our side. Purchases are the exception: those are recorded by Apple, not by us, and restoring them on a new device is handled through your Apple Account.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
