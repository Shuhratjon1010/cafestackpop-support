Privacy Policy

Cafe Stack Pop · last updated 28 July 2026

Cafe Stack Pop doesn't ask you to make an account, and it doesn't collect your name, your email address, or your phone number. Your game progress stays on your phone.

This policy explains the parts that aren't quite that simple — mainly advertising. It covers the Cafe Stack Pop iOS app, published by Daler Soliev.

What stays on your device

Your levels, stars, coins, hearts, cats, season progress, and settings are stored locally on your iPhone. We don't upload them, and we can't see them.

The practical consequence: if you delete the app, that progress is gone, and it won't follow you to a new phone. There's no server-side copy for us to restore.

Advertising

The game shows rewarded video ads — the kind you choose to watch in exchange for a booster, extra moves, or a heart refill. We use Google AdMob to serve them.

To do that, AdMob receives information from your device, which can include:

  • advertising and device identifiers
  • your device type, operating system version, and language
  • coarse location, inferred from your IP address
  • whether you started, finished, or closed an ad

Google is an independent controller of that data. What they do with it is governed by their own policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Before any of this is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites, iOS asks for your permission. If you say no, ads still work — they're just less targeted. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Cafe VIP subscribers see no ads, so none of this applies while a subscription is active.

Purchases

All purchases go through Apple's In-App Purchase system. Apple processes the payment and tells us only whether it succeeded. We never see your card number, your Apple Account credentials, or your billing address.

Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled. You manage and cancel them in your iPhone's Settings, not in the game — see the support page for the steps.

Children

Cafe Stack Pop is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages, but it isn't directed at children specifically and it isn't in the App Store's Kids Category. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the game contains ads and purchases, parents may want to turn on Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions to require approval before any purchase is made.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything the game has stored about you, because all of it lives on your device.

To reset advertising data, use Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking to withdraw permission, and Apple's Reset Advertising Identifier control.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we'll update this page and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
