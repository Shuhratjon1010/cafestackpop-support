Common questions

I bought something and didn't receive it.
Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your Apple account and re-grants anything already paid for. If it still hasn't appeared, email us with the date of the purchase.
How do I cancel Cafe VIP?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, pick Cafe Stack Pop, and tap Cancel. You keep VIP until the end of the period you've paid for.
I lost my progress.
Progress is stored on your device. Deleting the app removes it, and it won't transfer to a new phone on its own. Email us before you delete anything and we'll tell you what can be recovered.
Can I play without an internet connection?
Yes. Levels work fully offline. You'll need a connection for purchases and for timed events to update.
How do I ask for a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
