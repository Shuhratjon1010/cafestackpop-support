How to play a sorting puzzle game like Cafe Stack Pop

  1. Tap a treat. Only the front item on each shelf can move. Tap it and it lands in your tray.
  2. Match three to pop. The tray groups treats by type. When three of a kind meet, they pop and free up space.
  3. Mind the tray. You have 7 slots. Fill them without a match and the level ends, so plan a move ahead.
  4. Clear the shelves. Empty every shelf to win, earn up to 3 stars, and spend them decorating your cafe.

FAQ & support

How do you play Cafe Stack Pop?
Tap the front item on any shelf to move it into your 7-slot tray. When three matching treats meet in the tray they pop. Clear every shelf to finish the level, and keep the tray from filling up.
Is Cafe Stack Pop free?
Yes. All 120 levels are free to play. Optional in-app purchases add coins, boosters and a Cafe VIP subscription that removes ads and gives unlimited hearts.
Can I play offline?
Yes. Levels work fully offline. You only need a connection for purchases and for timed events to update.
I bought something and didn't receive it.
Open Settings inside the game and tap Restore Purchases. That re-checks your Apple account and re-grants anything already paid for. If it still hasn't appeared, email us with the date of the purchase.
How do I cancel Cafe VIP?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions, pick Cafe Stack Pop, and tap Cancel. You keep VIP until the end of the period you've paid for.
I lost my progress.
Progress is stored on your device. Use Back Up Save in Settings to copy a restore code before switching phones or deleting the app. Email us before you delete anything and we'll tell you what can be recovered.
How do I ask for a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.
Need a hand with anything else?
Email shuhrat.soliev@gmail.com. It helps if you include your device model, iOS version, and roughly what level you were on.
